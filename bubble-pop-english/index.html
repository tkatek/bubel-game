How to Play

  1. Read the question on the wooden banner at the top.
  2. Find the answer inside the bubbles — three other choices are hiding up there too.
  3. Aim and shoot with your mouse or finger. Bubbles bounce off the side walls!
  4. Match the colour: your shot must join 3 same-colour bubbles on the correct answer. Wrong shots cost a heart.
  5. Coloured obstacles are not answers. Pop a group of 3+ of their colour to clear them for bonus points — or aim around them. They fall when they lose their support!
  6. Watch the cannon: the loaded bubble and the Next preview show your upcoming colours. The queue always holds a colour that can win.
  7. Score and combos. Consecutive correct answers grow your multiplier, first-try answers and wall-bounce bank shots pay bonus points, and four shots for four answers earns a perfect-clear bonus. No mistakes = 3 stars!
  8. Power-ups: the rainbow bubble matches whatever it touches, the heart restores one life, and the eagle eye shows a longer aiming guide with the predicted landing spot.
